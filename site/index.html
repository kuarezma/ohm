What Ohm does

Battery receipt

Minutes and percent per app, per day. Measured, not guessed.

Efficiency lane

One click keeps an app on the E-cores. It switches back when you bring the app to the front.

Freeze and thaw

Hidden apps stop using power entirely. Ohm thaws them the moment you switch to them.

Runaway catcher

“node has used 94% CPU for 12 minutes in the background.” Act from the notification.

Rules in plain words

Type “When battery is under 30%, keep Chrome on efficiency cores.” Ohm turns it into a rule, on-device.

Widget and CLI

ohm receipt --today in your terminal, today's receipt on your desktop.

Why only Apple Silicon

Ohm uses cores and counters that only Apple Silicon has.

Separate performance and efficiency cores

Your Mac has performance cores and efficiency cores. Ohm can keep an app on the efficiency cores.

Per-process energy counters in the chip

The chip counts the energy each process uses. Ohm turns those counts into minutes of battery.

An on-device language model

Your sentence becomes a rule right on your Mac. Nothing goes to the cloud.

Zero Telemetry

0 bytes sent.

Ohm has no account, no analytics and no network access. The only exception is update checks, which you can turn off. The source is on GitHub.

Frequently asked questions

Honest answers regarding technical implementation and limitations.

Why is there an “Other” row?

The display and the radios cannot be attributed to a single app, so Ohm shows them separately instead of spreading them across your apps.

Does it need admin rights?

No, not for v1. Ohm reads the energy counters of your own apps without root, a helper or a kernel extension.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

No, Ohm reads counters that only Apple Silicon has.

Will freezing lose my work?

Ohm never freezes apps playing audio or in a call, and thaws everything if it quits or crashes.

What is the price?

Free and MIT-licensed.

Take control of Apple Silicon energy

Open source and light: Ohm itself runs on the efficiency cores and sends nothing anywhere.
